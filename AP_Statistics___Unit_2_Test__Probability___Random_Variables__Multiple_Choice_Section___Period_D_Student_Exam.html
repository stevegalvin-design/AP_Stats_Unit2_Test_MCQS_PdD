AP Statistics Formula Sheet (Unit 2: Probability Focus)

Probability Formulas:

• Conditional Probability: P(A|B) = P(A ∩ B) / P(B)

• Addition Rule: P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A ∩ B)

• Multiplication Rule (Independent Events): P(A ∩ B) = P(A) × P(B)

Discrete Random Variables:

• Mean / Expected Value: μ_X = E(X) = Σ [x_i × P(x_i)]

• Variance: σ_X² = Σ [(x_i - μ_X)² × P(x_i)]

Binomial Probability Distribution:

• P(X = k) = (n! / (k!(n-k)!)) × p^k × (1-p)^(n-k)

• Mean: μ_X = np  |  Standard Deviation: σ_X = √(np(1-p))
